Percentages

Build from 10% and 1%: 35% of 640 = 3×64 + ½×64 = 192 + 32 = 224.

Flip it: x% of y = y% of x. 8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2.

Remove GST: divide by 1.18, never multiply by 0.82.

Successive changes: a + b + ab/100. +20% then −20% = −4%.
